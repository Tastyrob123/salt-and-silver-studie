Invoicing.

Hospitality receipt or proper invoice, this is how it works.

To receive a hospitality receipt or a proper invoice, please send us an e-mail at: mail@saltandsilver.de with the following details:

  • Your company's billing address
  • Attach the hospitality receipt you received in the restaurant

Our team will then send you the requested invoice as a PDF. Please note that due to high demand the process can take up to a week.
