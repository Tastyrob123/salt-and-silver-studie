Description

Just like in recent years, this year again there is a tasty, sparkling and pretty Pet Nat from us and our friends Melanie and Michael from a wine and sparkling wine estate on the Moselle. Here you get, exclusively, the last of the bottles limited to 1000.

Pet Nat stands for Pétillant Naturel, a "naturally sparkling" wine from traditional bottle fermentation. The yeast stays in the bottle. That makes Pet Nat especially lively in taste.

From crisp, fresh herb and stone fruit notes to brioche and almonds: that comes from the Yellow Muscat grape and the steep slopes on the Moselle. Tobi and Jo trimmed, stripped leaves and picked themselves in autumn.
