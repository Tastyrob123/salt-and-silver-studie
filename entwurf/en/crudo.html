Salt & SilverClassics.

After all the culinary adventures, Bistro Crudo takes us back to our roots.

Here we serve tacos we like, ceviches we can be proud of, and bar food that makes you happy and thirsty. Whether a quick snack or a full table with lots of friends: Bistro Crudo is your weekend bistro for culinary trips around the world, Salt & Silver style.

So that things never get boring, we regularly host pop-ups here. Friends from all over the world cook their specialities and we take care of the good mood in the dining room. We announce the pop-ups in the newsletter and on social media.

From the cocktail bar come matching drinks with and without alcohol, plus a generous selection of very good wines, curated by head sommelier Tobias Beck.
