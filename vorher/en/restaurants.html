Hamburg

In Hamburg our two restaurant concepts are on St. Pauli Hafenstraße, by the Elbe with a view of the port and lots of sunny terrace seats. On the left in the basement is Bistro Crudo, on the right on the raised ground floor the Salt & Silver Restaurant.
