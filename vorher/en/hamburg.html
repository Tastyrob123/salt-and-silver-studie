Wanderlust cuisineà la carte.

A new chapter begins at Salt & Silver, with a strong team, new dishes and a whole lot of heart.

In the kitchen, Laura Kierse now stands at the stove as the new head chef together with Salt & Silver founder Jo. The two share the same passion: honest, precisely crafted cooking with soul.

Laura's Portuguese roots bring a new lightness and Mediterranean warmth to the dishes and meet Jo's typical wanderlust flavours, which came about around the world in ten years of Salt & Silver.

Together they cook a changing à la carte menu: regional ingredients, seasonal produce, fresh fish, game from Jagdgut Damp. In the dining room, sommelier Tobi Beck and Leni Knoll take care of the right pairing: fine wines, natural drinks, a warm atmosphere.

From far to table. Always with heart.
